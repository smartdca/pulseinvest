One order per asset, every trading day. Real money, no hindsight.

Real account record. Not a forecast, not investment advice. Updated each trading day after the US market closes.

1. Services: the broker is Alpaca (a live account, priced in US dollars). The multiplier comes from DCA Score, queried live before every order. A scheduled job on Vercel runs once every US trading day. Order records are kept in a Google Sheet and compiled into this page’s data after each trading day.

2. How we invest: every asset follows the same rules, with no per-asset parameters. Each trading day, one market order per asset, placed by dollar amount (stocks are day orders; crypto orders stay open until cancelled). The base amount is $1 per asset per day, the broker’s minimum for small orders, so we use the smallest amount to observe what the DCA Score multiplier does. The size of the amount is not the point. Amount invested = base amount × DCA Score multiplier, which is never below 1.0: it only boosts, it never reduces.

3. How the numbers are calculated: invested is the amount actually filled, usually about 1% to 2% below the ordered amount. Value is each trading day’s closing price × total quantity held. Return = (value − invested) ÷ invested. Boost rate = orders with a multiplier above 1.0 ÷ all filled orders. Data updates once per trading day, so this page shows the previous trading day’s results.

4. Costs and notes: stocks are commission-free; crypto carries a trading fee, which makes the filled quantity slightly smaller. The account number, cash balance and order IDs are not published.
